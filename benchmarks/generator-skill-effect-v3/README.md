# Generator skill-effect v3: does the generator's locator discipline survive a markup refactor, and which layer carries it?

Status: **`INCONCLUSIVE` (thin pool): after the preregistered third-repetition extension, the headroom screen kept 9 of 36 tasks against a minimum of 12, so the frozen rule prohibited the five-arm final comparison.** The no-skill screen completed 101/101 calls with no infrastructure error or timeout for $7.05. All 101 deliverables were green on the correct app; 37 survived the behavior-preserving markup refactor, and the tests killed 498/505 seeded faults. These are no-skill screening observations, not a skill comparison. No `full-fixed`, `full`, `lean`, or `rules-only` call ran, so v3 measures no generator-skill effect in either direction.

## Why v3

[v1](../generator-skill-effect-v1/README.md) and [v2](../generator-skill-effect-v2/README.md) were both `INCONCLUSIVE` for the same reason: on author-written small apps, `claude-sonnet-5-5` without the skill verifies nearly every stated behavior, so fault-kill rate has no headroom and cannot separate arms. What did vary, descriptively, in both runs is survival of a behavior-preserving markup refactor (v1: skill 23/30 against no-skill 12/28; v2 screen: 24/44 of the clean no-skill deliverables), and every observed failure was an `#id` or `.class` selector on an element whose role, label and text had not changed. v3 therefore promotes refactor survival to the primary metric, screens the pool for headroom on that metric with the no-skill arm only, and asks which layer of the skill carries the discipline: the current skill with its reachability fixes (`full-fixed`), the shipped 2.0.0 text (`full`), the rules plus a run-and-fix loop (`lean`), or the written locator and assertion rules alone (`rules-only`). The design is [`DESIGN.md`](DESIGN.md); the frozen contract is [`protocol.json`](protocol.json).

## Arms (digest-bound snapshots under `arms/`)

| Arm | Staged text | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `full-fixed` | `arms/full-fixed/snapshot/playwright-test-generator/`, the repository skill as of the GO snapshot (2.0.0 with items 1 to 6 applied), plus `arms/shared/e2e-reviewer` | the current product |
| `full` | `arms/full/playwright-test-generator/`, the 2.0.0 skill snapshot frozen for v2, plus `e2e-reviewer` | the shipped text before the fixes |
| `lean` | `arms/lean/SKILL.md`, v2's byte-frozen lean text, plus `e2e-reviewer` for its scanner step | rules plus a run-and-fix loop, without exploration, approval, V1 to V6 or the reviewer |
| `rules-only` | `arms/rules-only/RULES.md`, verbatim copies of five rule sections of the full-fixed text ([`TRACE.md`](arms/rules-only/TRACE.md), regenerated by `derive.py`) | whether the written rules alone are the active ingredient |
| `no-skill` | nothing | baseline and headroom screen |

No arm reads `skills/`; `selftest` proves it ([`reports/selftest.json`](reports/selftest.json)). Same host, model, tools, prompt frame, pre-granted approvals and project staging as v1 and v2.

## Pool, validation and screen

The pool contained 36 validated tasks: repaired copies of 22 usable v2 tasks and 14 new richer-markup tasks. Model-free validation reported zero problems, and the pool plus all five arm snapshots were digest-frozen at 2026-10-06T21:54:04Z before the first model call. The new tasks cover text-only counters and status lines, repeated components, nested controls, conditional regions, dialogs, tables, and labels supplied through `aria-labelledby`. For every task, validation proved that the author strong spec stayed green on the refactor while the id-based naive spec failed it.

## Decision rule (frozen before any call)

Primary: `full-fixed` versus `no-skill` on **refactor survival score** (1 when the delivered specs are green 3/3 on the correct app and green on the markup refactor, else 0). `PASS` needs paired lift >= +0.20, case-cluster bootstrap 95% CI lower bound > 0, the clean-pass guard (not more than 0.05 below no-skill), the fault-kill guard (effective fault-kill mean not more than 0.10 below no-skill with the paired CI lower bound above -0.10), and a kept-set no-skill primary below 0.85; `INCONCLUSIVE` on any unscored call, drift, incomplete schedule or ceiling; otherwise `FAIL`. Preregistered secondaries: `full`, `lean` and `rules-only` each versus `no-skill` under the same rule; `lean` and `rules-only` non-inferior to `full-fixed` (margin 0.10 on the mean, paired CI lower bound above -0.10, clean-pass guard); `full-fixed` versus `full` on the transcript-derived legitimate-Complete and V6-verbatim-verdict rates (+0.30, CI lower bound > 0); locator census, scanner counts, cost and time multiples reported without thresholds. No spend cap (user decision); every cost is recorded. Power: with 12 kept tasks and an assumed paired-difference SD of 0.35 the minimum detectable lift is about 0.28 at 80% power; a `FAIL` means "no large effect shown" and a non-inferiority `PASS` means "no large deficit shown".

## Screen

The screen ran from 2026-10-06T21:54Z until the selection freeze at 2026-10-07T05:15:53Z. The first stage ran the no-skill arm twice on all 36 tasks (72/72 calls, $5.05). Seven tasks had a mean refactor score of 0.50, below the minimum of 12, so the preregistered extension ran a third no-skill repetition on every one of the 29 tasks outside the band (29/29 calls, $2.00). Reapplying the frozen [0.25, 0.75] band to all repetitions kept nine tasks: `v2-01`, `v2-06`, `v2-08`, `v2-11`, `v2-13`, `v2-16`, `v2-20`, `v3-06`, and `v3-12`. Their screening means were 0.3333, 0.5, 0.5, 0.5, 0.5, 0.6667, 0.5, 0.5, and 0.5 respectively. Nine tasks were at 1.0 and 18 were at 0.0 after the extension. Nine is below the frozen minimum of 12, so the protocol stopped without relaxing the band or running a skilled arm.

Raw calls and deterministic scores are [`reports/screen.json`](reports/screen.json), [`reports/screen-scores.json`](reports/screen-scores.json), [`reports/screen-extend.json`](reports/screen-extend.json), and [`reports/screen-extend-scores.json`](reports/screen-extend-scores.json). The selection, report digests, score digests, and $7.0518 spend are frozen in `protocol.json` `screening` and copied to [`reports/screening.json`](reports/screening.json).

Across the 101 no-skill screening calls, all 101 deliverables were green in three of three clean runs; 37 survived the markup refactor, for a refactor score of 0.366; and the specs killed 498/505 seeded faults, for a mean effective kill rate of 0.986. The scanner reported 0 P0 and 15 P1/P2 heuristic hits. Transcripts show that 70/101 calls ran Playwright Test, 52 ran `--repeat-each`, 63 read `app.mjs`, four ran `git status`, and none ran the scanner, a browser-exploration command, or a subagent. These values are descriptive screen data only.

## Results

Not run. The frozen rule required at least 12 kept tasks before any skilled call; nine qualified after the only permitted extension. No `full-fixed`, `full`, `lean`, or `rules-only` call was made, so there is no lift, confidence interval, non-inferiority result, completion-rate comparison, or cost multiple for those arms. Machine-readable verdict: [`reports/results.json`](reports/results.json).

Interpretation: changing the primary from fault-kill to markup-refactor survival did create variance, but the prespecified band still did not produce a sufficiently large comparison set. In most tasks, every observed repetition either survived or failed for this model rather than landing in the desired middle band. This protocol therefore cannot answer whether the full skill, its reachability fixes, a lean loop, or the written rules improve refactor robustness. No skill effect is claimed in either direction.

Post-hoc design implication: excluding every 0.0 task removed 18 tasks on which no-skill had the most room to improve, even though each task's author strong spec survived the same refactor. A successor protocol should preregister either a comparison over the full validated pool or a floor/middle/ceiling stratified sample instead of requiring a minimum number of middle-band tasks. That recommendation does not alter this frozen selection or turn its screen into a skill comparison.

## Limits

- One host and model family; author-written apps; one author-written refactor per task, so the primary measures survival of that rename, not general locator robustness. The refactor keeps roles, labels, names and text, so `getByText` on a renamed id survives by construction: the metric rewards exactly the discipline the rules teach and says nothing about locators that break when visible text changes.
- `lean` and `rules-only` are deletions of the real skill and are not shippable; a null difference against `full-fixed` is bounded by power, not proof of equivalence.
- Headless skill behavior with pre-granted approvals is measured; the interactive approval gate is not. `legitimate_complete` is a transcript heuristic.
- The screen selects on one model's no-skill locator habits; the kept set says nothing about other models, and regression toward the pool mean in the fresh repetitions is reported, not corrected.
- The minimum of 12 was not met, so no case-cluster confidence interval or arm comparison exists. The nine kept tasks are screening evidence only, not an underpowered final comparison.
- The 1-minute host load threshold was raised from 12 to 20 before the remaining run, as recorded in `protocol.json`. Sixty-nine of 101 model calls started above load 12, none above 20. The runner paused 13 times for 1,380 seconds. Of 909 deterministic Playwright scoring runs, 170 started above load 12. No model call, scoring run, or retry was classified as an infrastructure error, and no load-caused retry occurred.
- All 101 no-skill deliverables were green on the correct app, so this screen does not measure generator reachability or completion reporting under a skill. The four skilled snapshots remain frozen inputs for provenance, not evaluated arms.

## Reproduce

```bash
npm ci --prefix scripts/evals/fixtures
R=benchmarks/generator-skill-effect-v3/reports
python3 scripts/evals/run-generator-skill-effect-v3.py validate --output $R/validation.json
python3 scripts/evals/run-generator-skill-effect-v3.py selftest --output $R/selftest.json
python3 scripts/evals/run-generator-skill-effect-v3.py freeze --stage pool --validation $R/validation.json
python3 scripts/evals/run-generator-skill-effect-v3.py screen --output $R/screen.json --allow-live
python3 scripts/evals/run-generator-skill-effect-v3.py score $R/screen.json --output $R/screen-scores.json
# only when fewer than 12 tasks sit in the band after two repetitions:
python3 scripts/evals/run-generator-skill-effect-v3.py screen --extend $R/screen-scores.json --output $R/screen-extend.json --allow-live
python3 scripts/evals/run-generator-skill-effect-v3.py score $R/screen-extend.json --output $R/screen-extend-scores.json
python3 scripts/evals/run-generator-skill-effect-v3.py select --scores $R/screen-scores.json [--extend-scores $R/screen-extend-scores.json]
python3 scripts/evals/run-generator-skill-effect-v3.py run --output $R/final.json --allow-live
python3 scripts/evals/run-generator-skill-effect-v3.py score $R/final.json --output $R/final-scores.json
python3 scripts/evals/run-generator-skill-effect-v3.py analyze $R/final-scores.json --output $R/final-results.json
```

`screen` and `run` refuse to start on digest drift or an unfrozen prior stage; `select` refuses scores that did not come from a `no-skill` screen; delivered files are archived under `reports/*-artifacts/`, so `score` can rerun without model calls.
