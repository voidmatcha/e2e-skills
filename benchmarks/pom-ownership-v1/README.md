# Page Object ownership validation v1

Status: **SUPERSEDED PREPARATION; no measured comparison or generalization claim**. The changed collector has a separate [v2 protocol](../pom-ownership-v2/protocol.json); do not run or pool this v1 matrix with it.

The [protocol](protocol.json) fixes the question and decision rule before another model run. The treatment is the new mandatory `#13` ownership-lead surface; the control must be an otherwise byte-identical skill with only that feature disabled. This isolates the collector from the other changes between the earlier Appsmith runs. The existing [Appsmith follow-up](../official-skills-pilot/README.md) is development evidence and is excluded from this study.

The primary population is individual named Cypress specs under `cypress/e2e/` with the relevant `cypress/support/` source available. Directory scans, other layouts, computed selectors, and Playwright are not covered by the automatic invocation and will not be pooled into this result. A zero-lead scanner output is not a clean `#13` verdict. Reviewers must apply the [source-ownership contract](../../skills/e2e-reviewer/references/pattern-reference.md) before labeling a finding.

Before the first measured call, the remaining preparation gates are: select and pin source-complete real cases from at least ten repositories; independently adjudicate 20 positive workflows, their 20 controlled repairs, and 20 unrelated clean controls; archive the two exact arm trees and their diff; validate an isolated runner and scorer; freeze all hashes and the schedule. A separate 12-input pilot may test plumbing only. Neither pilot outcomes nor the existing Appsmith result may be counted in the confirmatory score. The 360-call matrix is intentionally not authorized by this preparation record alone; its estimated usage and budget cap must be set before execution.

Initial public GitHub source screening is recorded in [screening.json](screening.json). It is exploratory source discovery, not a labeled case list or model result. Repositories whose source was inspected for development cannot be counted as fresh confirmatory cases. The case inventory and label adjudication are still pending; this study is **not frozen or runnable**.

The first five screened repositories yielded no confirmed natural positive. One produced several same-selector leads whose ownership is not established by the selector match, and one named spec exceeded the collector's 20-lead cap and returned `INCOMPLETE`. This is a feasibility warning, not a measured recall or false-positive result. Do not fill the primary positive quota with those unadjudicated leads or silently replace natural cases with authored mutants.

Relaxing discovery to Playwright specs and Page Objects outside the Cypress collector's supported layout produced two source-supported natural `#13` candidates, in OHIF/Viewers and AdaptiveConsulting/ReactiveTraderCloud (anchors in [screening.json](screening.json)). The named-file scanner completed on both but did not surface `#13`, as expected for this separate layout. These examples show that broader semantic review can find bypasses; they do **not** establish any recall improvement from the Cypress automatic lead collector. Both repositories were inspected during development, so neither can enter the fresh confirmatory corpus. Any Playwright extension needs a separate preregistered matrix and treatment definition; do not pool it with the primary result.

A nonstandard Cypress layout in harvester/tests yielded a useful **debatable** case: a raw Create click matches a Page Object's `save()` click, but the test needs the response body that `save()` does not expose. Directly replacing the click with `save()` would be wrong; the existing same-page create workflow may still establish ownership under #13's broader rule. Do not score this as a negative or positive without further adjudication. This inspected repository likewise cannot enter the fresh primary corpus.

## Focused development A/B, not the registered study

After the broader search, no **new source-confirmed natural positive inside the automatic Cypress population** was established. The expanded [screening ledger](screening.json) records the repositories and the reasons same-selector or out-of-layout cases were excluded. GitHub search is relevance-ordered and this was not exhaustive source sampling; the observation is a feasibility limit, not a prevalence estimate.

A follow-up sweep of local pinned sources found two more source-supported natural Cypress candidates, but neither enters that population: xchem's `smoke.cy.js` clicks a menu button already exposed and used through `General.elements.openMenuButton` in another spec; RedHat's skipped `RosaClusterWizard.js` duplicates wizard clicks implemented and used through `CreateRosaWizardPage`. Both Page Objects live under `cypress/pageobjects/`, not the automatic `cypress/support/` root. The xchem `body`/Escape match is a different-purpose negative guard. These are discovery candidates, not independent labels or new model results. Combined with the earlier out-of-layout cases, the immediate engineering question is **context-root coverage before AST precision**; changing the parser alone would not surface either automatic miss.

The expanded [hard-case screening](hard-cases.json) consolidates 15 source-anchored development clusters across seven pinned repositories: five positive candidates, five negative guards, and five cases requiring further judgment. Repeated lines from one mechanism count once. An independent read-only review corrected several initial classifications, including Harvester's response-dependent create flow, which is debatable rather than a proven negative. These are neither gold labels nor 15 independent failure mechanisms; none adds a confirmed positive to the current automatic Cypress population. Do not use these inspected sources as fresh confirmatory cases.

We therefore ran a small **feature-only diagnostic** on the already-known Appsmith S3 BEFORE/AFTER pair. The historical [ablation builder](../../scripts/evals/make-pom-ownership-control.py) removes the automatic lead pass, helper, and point-of-use instructions from the evaluated treatment snapshot while retaining the same `#13` ownership rule and all other review behavior. It cannot be run against the current checkout after the default scanner stopped invoking the collector; use the recorded arm digests, not a newly built current-tree arm, to identify that pilot. The runner used fresh opaque workspaces and sessions, the same prompt and source snapshots, reversed arm order in repetition two, and verified unchanged source and skill digests. The [cell ledger](development-pilot.json) records all eight call timings, token counters, result hashes, and exact arm/checkout digests; raw events remain in local temporary storage and are not independently replayable from this repository.

| Appsmith `#13` target | Control, no leads | Treatment, leads |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| BEFORE, correct source-anchored hit | 0/2 | 2/2 |
| AFTER, same-target alarm | 0/2 | 0/2 |

Across four calls per arm, treatment recorded 1,941,360 versus 2,260,740 input tokens and 410.6 versus 467.1 summed call-seconds. **Do not interpret those lower observed totals as a cost or speed improvement**: one pair and two stochastic repeats cannot isolate those effects, and input includes cached tokens rather than billed usage. All eight reports also raised `#4i`, `#5b`, and `#9b`. `#10f` appeared once in each arm on different sides; the cited chains exist, but other findings were not exhaustively adjudicated, so this is not a whole-report accuracy comparison.

The diagnostic supports only that the automatic lead surface changed this known case under the fixed skill snapshot. It does not satisfy the registered ten-repository, 20-positive study or prove a general recall gain. Do not scale to the 360-call matrix until fresh eligible natural cases and independent labels exist. If those cases remain scarce, narrow the product claim instead of filling the quota with authored mutants or out-of-layout Playwright examples.

The preregistered decision is at least a 20-point stable `#13` recall gain, a repository-clustered 95% interval entirely above zero, gains from at least three repositories, and no additional stable `#13` alarms on repaired or clean controls. All newly reported non-`#13` findings also require adjudication. This is a deliberately demanding public-development test, not a sealed release certification; a complete negative result stays negative, and an incomplete matrix remains `INCOMPLETE`.
